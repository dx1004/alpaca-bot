## $500 affordability stress examples

The following are **invented arithmetic scenarios**, not historical fills, a position recommendation, a cash-settlement model, or Ross risk parameters. Assume the stated available amount is independently verified, no borrowing, whole shares solely for these examples, and zero fees solely to isolate arithmetic. Any fees/reserves reduce capacity. Affordability alone never authorizes an entry.

| Scenario | Deterministic calculation | Research implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $500 available, buy limit $5.05 | floor(500 / 5.05) = 99 shares; maximum limit-price notional $499.95; 100 shares would require $505 | Last-trade price is not the requested limit-price budget. This is an arithmetic ceiling, not an approved size. |
| $200 already reserved from that $500 budget | floor((500 - 200) / 5.05) = 59 shares; $297.95 | Use a consistent budget basis; do not subtract reservations twice from broker-reported available buying power. |
| Buy 100 at ask $5.00, hypothetical immediate sell at bid $4.90 | 100 × ($5.00 - $4.90) = $10, or 2% of $500 | A quoted spread can materially affect a small account; neither side's quantity or fill is assumed guaranteed. No spread veto threshold is inferred. |
| 60 actual shares, hypothetical stop reference $4.80, exit $4.55 | 60 × ($4.80 - $4.55) = $15 additional adverse difference | A reference stop does not bound executed loss. This is not the UAVS trade or a calibrated slippage distribution. |
| 99 actual shares with a source-specific 50% or 75% partial | 49.5 or 74.25 shares respectively | Integer rounding changes management. Fractional execution or rounding cannot be silently chosen to combine distinct source versions. |

The unresolved risk allowance and sourced stop would impose an additional bound beyond affordability. No risk fraction is selected here. User-authorized capital stages remain unchanged: no borrowed leverage below $2,000; future leveraged rules require separate broker/version evidence.

## Source-scoped research labels and news handling

This is an engineering annotation specification for the next offline study, **not an executable Ross selector**. Use `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `OUT_OF_SCOPE` per proposition. A case can support one proposition and leave the others unresolved.

1. **Version and applicability:** source ID/locator, setup family, account phase, session date and date-evidence status. Do not merge one-minute micro, five-minute flag and HOD-break examples.
2. **Observation availability:** original event time, publisher time/time zone, provider receipt, system first-seen, and decision time, each independently nullable. Publication time alone cannot prove availability to the trader/system. Unknown availability cannot pass a historical timing claim.
3. **News identity and history:** issuer/security mapping as of the session, original announcement, financial terms, correction/supersession links, source coverage and raw evidence reference. Distinguish Ross reporting no news from a failed/unavailable search. A later filing cannot overwrite the earlier knowledge state.
4. **Separate facts and judgment:** Ross's catalyst assessment and hot/cold description, whether retrospective, the cited chart/depth observations, and the stated reason. AI may propose evidence-linked extraction for review; its sentiment or confidence cannot substitute for missing source rules, first-seen data, or deterministic checks. No universal “24-hour news” rule, score threshold, or offering ban is established.
5. **Actions and fills:** abstained, missed, prepared, submitted, partially filled, cancelled and exited are distinct. Record actual known quantity separately from intended size and preserve unknown account attribution. “Did not trade” must not be relabelled a missed profitable entry solely from later price.
6. **Outcome and eligibility:** keep realized/reported outcome in a separate field. Have reviewers label eligibility from available-at-decision evidence before outcomes in a future unseen study. These already read recaps cannot be used to claim blinded performance.

Concrete review checks: C10 must not create a cross-version no-news exception; C11 must retain the BXG abstention and distinguish its position-cap rejection from cash; C12 must keep publisher/first-seen/closing times separate; C13 must not become a micro-pullback or a hindsight regime rule; C14 must not turn a prepared add into a fill. The arithmetic checks must retain unknown risk and non-guaranteed execution. These are fidelity checks, not newly optimized trading filters.
