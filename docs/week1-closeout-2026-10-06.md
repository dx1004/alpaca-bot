## Additional primary-source findings from the closeout review

- **Issuer news timing:** SEC's [Webmaster FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions) says EDGAR filings are often on sec.gov 1–3 minutes after acceptance, possibly longer with load and with no guarantee; it explicitly says there is no timestamp for when filing content first becomes available on sec.gov. SEC says public EDGAR filing content is free to access and reuse. This can support a dated issuer-disclosure archive, but not a news-provider/system-first-seen clock or complete catalyst feed.
- **Ross scanner history and latency:** Warrior's [scanner documentation](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000117763) describes current top-gap lists as the top 100 and refreshed every 30 seconds, stopping at 9:30 a.m. ET; its scanner-history widget covers only the prior six months. The news indicator on list scanners updates with the 30-second refresh, while alert-scanner news indicators may lag the alert by up to five minutes. These are useful current semantics, not scanner settings/history for the 2017 or 2021 exposed sessions.
- **Depth:** Nasdaq's [Historical ITCH product directory](https://beta.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=MDReports) describes T+1 order/trade logs from Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH and secured website/FTP access. This is Nasdaq-venue data, not consolidated all-venue depth; neither free access nor the exact target files/rights was verified.
- **Float:** AHPI's [2020 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874710/000110465920109282/tm2024734d1_10k.htm) gives common shares outstanding as of 2020-09-16. That is not a scanner float for the 2021 trade date; no session-specific free historical float snapshot was found for the exposed symbols. Shares outstanding cannot be substituted for float.
- **Source-collection terms:** Warrior's [current terms](https://www.warriortrading.com/disclaimer/) state that automated access/harvesting is prohibited and grant a limited, revocable personal-use license. Do not build a scraper or bulk downloader for Warrior content absent an authorized acquisition path; public page visibility alone does not verify automated extraction or long-term archive rights. This is a source-use constraint, not evidence that the already-recorded Ross statements are strategy rules.

The independent `gpt-6-astra` / `ultra` primary-source pass found no free public source that closes these replay gaps. EDGAR public filings can be reused, but SEC does not record the exact first-public-availability timestamp; its typical 1–3 minute acceptance lag is not guaranteed. Warrior's current scanner history reaches only six months, too short for the 2017/2021 sessions, and does not establish the historical scanner configuration. Nasdaq Historical ITCH is described as secured T+1 Nasdaq-only order/trade data, not all-venue depth. Issuer shares outstanding cannot substitute for historical float. Both Ross sessions remain exposed development cases, not holdout candidates. The 2026-10-06 Webull public-source pass also found a documented non-display Nasdaq Basic/TotalView OpenAPI route, but no verified CTA/UTP SIP equivalence, exact account price/license or complete news/float semantics; it cannot close the current Ross scanner path.
