## Identify the setup by name and source version

The [2025 Ross small-account toolkit](https://cdn.warriortrading.com/warriortrading.com/assets/2025-Small-Account-Tool-Kit-1013.pdf#page=4) calls its primary front-side micro-pullback setup the pattern described in Chapter 8 of *Strategies & Scaling*. The [Warrior support article](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000160677-macd-resources) links “The 1st and 2nd Pullbacks” under Chapter 8 and says that video is Pro-only, outside a trial. The [current syllabus](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000071156) instead places first/second pullbacks and micro-pullbacks in Chapter 7; its Chapter 8 is Gap & Go. “Chapter 8” is therefore a historical alias, not a unique rule version. The member lesson itself has not been accessed.

Research decision: public original material can support **individual, cited rules** and reasoned hypotheses about what the historical lesson likely covered. It cannot establish that the reconstruction contains everything taught in the inaccessible lesson. Use the setup name and source version, not the chapter number, as the unit of comparison. Record each proposed part of the reconstruction as `ROSS_DIRECT_PUBLIC` (stated in a public Ross source), `ROSS_INFERRED` (a documented inference across Ross sources), `OTHER_AUTHOR_ANALOGY` (a separately attributed comparison), or `UNKNOWN` (no defensible content). These are research labels, not fidelity-matrix rule classes. For every inference, show the source chain, alternative interpretation, version/account-stage fit and a way to falsify it. A missing numerical or discretionary condition may be hypothesized in the research record but remains `BLOCKED_RESEARCH` for operational use until separately accepted. No membership purchase is required to complete this bounded public-source research.

Research completion means that accessible original sources and relevant similar cases have been surveyed, the likely content and competing possibilities are mapped by setup and version, material gaps are explicit, and the resulting hypotheses have a predeclared offline validation plan. It does **not** mean exact recovery, proof of Ross endorsement, profitability, or authority to turn an inferred risk/entry/exit rule into an automated order. The separate executable and paper-release gates still apply.

The [R0 public pullback research package](ross-public-pullback-r0.md), reviewed on 2026-09-30, now records 16 source-scoped propositions, five documentary teaching cases, $500 affordability arithmetic and an ordered closure queue. It supersedes broad first-pass claims that no public candle/depth/MACD semantics exist: some are documented within named versions, although a complete executable profile is still unavailable. No other-trader parameters were adopted in R0.

## Candidate split and unresolved choices

1. **Ross-public baseline:** only statements directly supported by public Ross sources, with their version and scope. Ambiguous setup, risk, add, exit, and daily-stop transitions remain `BLOCKED_RESEARCH` and generate no order.
2. **Hybrid candidates:** isolate any non-Ross rule as a separately named hypothesis with its author, exact source, intended gap, operational definition, and explicit difference from the Ross baseline. Do not put it in `evidence/fidelity-matrix.yaml` as `ROSS_DIRECT`. Compare candidates one change at a time; do not combine several changes and attribute an outcome to one source.
3. **Engineering controls:** $500 starting equity, maximum 1× broker-reported buying power, eligible symbols, settled/available funds, source freshness, data coverage, paper-only endpoint, and durable reconciliation. These may veto exposure but do not create signals or select winners.

The first research questions are: what counts as the first or second pullback; what measurable event starts and invalidates each; which completed candle and volume comparison a trigger uses; whether Level 2/tape judgment is essential; how stops, partial sales, adds, and daily loss rules apply at $500/1×; and whether the available free data can reconstruct the candidate set at the actual decision time. No answer may be supplied by a backtest's best-performing parameter.

An arithmetic-only check shows why account stage matters: with exactly $500 available and whole shares, the upper bound is 100 shares at $5, 50 at $10, and 25 at $20, before any broker hold or price movement. This verifies purchasing-power arithmetic only. It does **not** choose a position size, approve a stop/risk profile, or show that any setup is tradable. Actual replay must use the then-current broker-reported available amount and asset eligibility.

For zero-subscription replay, [Alpaca Basic](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api) advertises historical equities back to 2016, but Basic's current 15 minutes are restricted and its live equity feed is IEX only. Alpaca describes [IEX historical data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-stock-data-1) as a single-exchange feed rather than a full-market record. It also requires authentication, and the paper credentials are not configured here. Once available, this can test mechanics on labeled symbols with a declared `NOT_SIP_EQUIVALENT` limitation; it cannot validate full-market discovery, relative volume, or first-seen news timing by itself. The data source and its account entitlement must be checked before any replay result is accepted.

Broker adaptation: [Alpaca's support answer](https://alpaca.markets/support/alpaca-cash-accounts) says accounts below $2,000 use limited margin: proceeds from sales may be reused before settlement, although [borrowing leverage remains unavailable below $2,000](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling). Its [current non-leverage margin FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/understanding-the-new-intraday-margin-rule) says the former PDT trade-count restriction has been removed, subject to equity and intraday risk checks. Ross's cash-account scarcity is therefore context for his stock-selection discipline, not an automatic one-trade-per-day Alpaca constraint. The actual paper account's buying power and restrictions still require a read-only probe.

User-confirmed capital boundary: below $2,000, keep this $500-origin stage at no borrowed leverage. At or above $2,000, a separate Ross leveraged version is acceptable for research and later paper evaluation, subject to source/version linkage and actual Alpaca account eligibility; crossing the threshold does not switch the bot automatically. The [2025 Ross $2,000 challenge](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000168902-small-account-challenges-at-warrior-trading) began at CMEG with 6x available and later reset to $2,000 without leverage. The $2,000 Webull cash-account context in the article above is a different example. [Alpaca's account fields](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-plans) list 1x, 2x and 4x, while its [margin rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling) set the $2,000 equity prerequisite and say 4x intraday may be allowed. None of these establishes a transferable 6x Ross execution profile for Alpaca.

## Validation sequence and acceptance evidence

1. **Source audit:** for each candidate, save source URL/access date, author, version, exact supported proposition, unresolved interpretation, and whether it is Ross-public, other-trader, broker, or engineering evidence. A source's example is not a universal rule. Freeze definitions before outcome testing.
2. **Offline labeled replay:** assemble contemporaneous positive, negative, and ambiguous examples without future bars or revised news. Keep a separate untouched holdout. Compare candidate detection to source examples and manually review disagreements. Report missing inputs, false positives, false negatives, and abstentions; a profitable example cannot validate attribution.
3. **$500 / 1× feasibility:** replay a $500 account ledger with actual broker symbol eligibility, available buying power and settlement semantics. Calculate the affordable-share ceiling separately. Derive proposed share count deterministically only after the candidate's stop and risk rule are sourced, then cap it by verified 1× buying power; reject zero shares or an unsourced risk rule. Report opportunities lost to capital, spread, fees, partial fills and missing data. Never simulate 6× or silently resize a Ross example and call it equivalent.
4. **Prospective shadow, then paper:** after source and data gates, capture live-time decisions without orders; reconcile feed latency, news first-seen time and account state. Paper orders belong only to a separately accepted release and the already planned paper window. Preserve `NOT_SIP_EQUIVALENT` when free feeds lack full-market coverage. A hybrid paper report must be labeled hybrid and cannot be used as evidence of Ross fidelity.
