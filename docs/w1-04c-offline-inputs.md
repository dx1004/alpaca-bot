## Comparison and holdout protocol

The unit is **proposition × case × source family**, with source and independent reviewer labels in `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNRESOLVED`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`; keep a reason and the evidence available at `t`. Compare labels before considering the later outcome. Report a full confusion/disagreement table, unresolved-input counts and reviewer rationale. A source-stated ordinal can be reproduced as a documentary label, while the same case's trigger, risk or exit may remain unresolved. No rule should be fitted to make the already-read winners or losers look consistent.

Before acquiring replay data, freeze each sufficiently defined proposition's reference bar, timeframe, availability cutoff, session and abstention logic. Reserve unseen **sessions**, not individual trades from an already-read session, by a fixed inclusion procedure and data-source availability check made before outcome review. Keep all source-reported examples above in the development set. With only one source-image session label, no complete point-in-time input set and no independent unseen sessions, the holdout is presently **empty**, and a reproducible time-series source-label comparison cannot yet be accepted. Do not report agreement or P&L from these documentary examples as replay validation.

Next closure evidence: a first-party exact-session identifier for at least one eligible source case, verified lawful historical provider/account access and data fields, a second independent label pass on timestamped inputs, and a precommitted separate session set. The latest-data authentication check does not establish historical replay rights or coverage. Missing prerequisites block W1-04c; they do not reopen the completed bounded W1-04b research deliverable or authorize strategy paper orders.
